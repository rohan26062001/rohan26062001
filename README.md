## 🎓 About Me

Hello, I'm Rohan, from Howrah, West Bengal. Currently, I work as a Software Engineer at **eQ Technologic**. I pursued my bachelor's degree in Information Technology from the **Institute of Engineering and Management (IEM), Kolkata** and completed my schooling at **Belur High School (H.S.)**, which helped shape my character.

I'm a technology enthusiast with a deep interest in **Web Development**, **Machine Learning**, and **Blockchain**. Along with that, I'm passionate about **finance**, believing that it plays a crucial role in shaping any company's backbone. I aspire to create an impact on **human ergonomics** using technology by developing meaningful solutions.

### 🌱 Involvement
I'm also attached to the **NGO JCP Foundation**, where I teach **elementary Mathematics, Physics**, and **English** to needy students in Liluah, Howrah. It's a pro bono service aimed at strengthening their foundations in these subjects, benefiting them throughout their education and career. Most of the students are from Bengali-medium schools, so I focus on their English as well.

### ⚡ Fun Fact
I love watching **stand-up comedy**, listening to **Rabindra Sangeet**, and doing **Recitation**!
